## How to read this record

- **Baseline**: direct radix 13 carry form on both operands, without completion. This is the 9-product construction cited in earlier documents.
- **Speed ratio**: baseline product count divided by this construction's cost. A value greater than 1 favors this construction.
- Cost is expressed in FP4 product units: 1 product costs 1.0. A product using a different carrier costs R_4/R_carrier units (equation (5)).
- A **plane** is an operand formed as a linear combination of limbs. Main planes use FP4; correction planes (completion Δ and ternary terms) can be combined into 1 matrix.
- Interpolation coefficients are only ±1 for every construction here, so **(P1)** holds (reconstruction from lattice GEMM). No construction requires (P2).

## High-radix limbs with gap completion (radix 15/17)

Premise: the conclusion that mixed radix offers no benefit applies to **exhaustive enumeration without correction (limb-value set ⊆S)**.
High radices with gap completion lie outside that result. They were added to the catalogue and enumeration was repeated.

The E2M1 lattice S lacks the signed limb values ±5, ±7. These 2 gaps can **both be filled with +1**
(5=4+1, 7=6+1; both 4 and 6 belong to S). Thus the Δ plane needs only the value set {0,±1},
just 1 ternary type: 1 plane per position, or multiple positions combined as Σδ_i b^i into 1 plane.

## Search cutoffs (explicit scope restrictions)

- Per-operand catalogue: (a) carry without correction: radices 2..13 × max|c|∈{1,2,3,4,6,8,12} × carry/split_all/split_top × with/without a ternary plane, up to 16 limbs; (b) high radix with gap completion: radices 5..17 × the same caps × all positions/top only × mixed radix (only the top ratio differs), up to 5 limbs (exact enumeration).
- Δ plane values are fixed to {0,±1}, since gaps ±5,±7 are both filled using +1. Completion using ±2 or larger was not explored.
- Correction planes are only Δ (completion) and ternary planes. To store the partner in 1 plane, carrier eligibility uses contiguous integer ranges {'fp8': 16, 'int8': 127, 'bf16': 256, 'fp16': 2048}.
- Reductions (Karatsuba and Toom) are handled separately in stage 5; this plane uses only direct plane pairs.
- Accumulator conditions use fixed K=16384.
- e2e fixed costs use linear interpolation from sm_120, round 20260817, N=8192, with 4 measurements (GEMM/e2e × p=2,3): C_fix=0.4758+0.0346×plane count [BF16 time]. No extrapolation to other N or hardware.
- FP4 per-product cost is calibrated at p=2 (0.240154 BF16 time). Calibration at p=3 gives 0.210438, increasing relative correction cost by approximately 14%; this is recorded as sensitivity.
- V9 checks only whether the radix is representable in scales. Effects of TMEM column count and concurrent MMA operations on B300 are unmeasured and not quantified.
- Stage 2: plane components in [-4,4], ||u||_1<=4. Values of 5 or greater are handled separately in stage 5.
- Stage 2: counterexample search for the rule restricts components to [-3,3], ||u||_1<=6, and contiguous limb-value sets.
- Stage 4: exhaustive mixed-radix search uses radices 2..13 and up to 5 limbs. For p>5, only uniform radix is considered, justified by the counting upper bound.
- Stage 4: uniform radix at p>=6 is extrapolated using the T3 recurrence; p<=5 agrees with exact enumeration.
- Reductions are limited to direct (L=2..5), Karatsuba depth 1 (L=2,4) and depth 2 (L=4), and Toom-2.5 (3,2). Radices 2..13.
- The limb-value pool is all of S (15 values). Per-position restrictions were not explored: they only reduce coverage and cannot improve feasibility.
- Question b: weight bounds m1<=24, m2<=260, m3<=1400 (m1<=m2<=m3).
- Question b pruning: |R2|>=137 (15 blocks must cover 2047 integers), m3 <= 2*max|R2|+1, and 12*(m1+m2+m3) >= 1023.
